      Next 24-hour supply-and-demand estimate

      This is a 24-hour total, not a guarantee of continuous water availability. A daily surplus can still coincide with a shortage if hourly demand exceeds incoming supply and the tank runs low.

      Check against real readings

      Read the tank level twice (no tanker delivery in between). If it drains faster than the model expects, the gap is a possible leak.

      How it works. A transparent water-balance model, not a black box: hourly level = level + supply − use − leak. Use = people × baseline consumption (135 L/person/day assumed, editable) × heat factor (+1.5% per °C above 30). Assumptions: 35% of use is non-essential and can be cut by 40%; suitable greywater reuse trims fresh-water use by 10% (illustrative assumption). The 96-hour crisis score is based on the no-action forecast: 100 × (1 − hours to empty ÷ 96), bounded between 0 and 100. It reaches 0 when the no-action forecast is 96 hours or longer; the action-adjusted empty time is shown separately. Tanker is scheduled to arrive 6 h before predicted empty. All assumptions are yours to change; replace them with your site's real numbers.
